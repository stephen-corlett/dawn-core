# Zone brief: <name>

Copy this to `docs/world/zones/<name>.md`. See `docs/zone-pipeline.md`. Paths here aren't links, so
that they still work once copied. Keep it short, because a brief that needs reading twice won't be
followed.

## Premise

Gate 1. Keep this to a page.

- **Request:** What was asked for, in the requester's own words.
- **Theme:** What life is like here, in a line, such as a struggle the people share. A theme isn't a
  storyline.
- **Threads:** The small, separate troubles that come from the theme, each of which can carry quests.
- **What happened here:** The history, in a paragraph. It explains why the land looks as it does.
- **Who lives here:** Each group, what they want, and what that does to the land.
- **The feeling:** One sentence. Then how it should change from arrival to the far end.
- **Place in the world:** Its row in `docs/world/atlas.md`: level range, speed, connections.
- **Mood:** Palette, light, and fog, for the zone's section of `docs/art/bible.md`.
