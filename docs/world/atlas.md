# World atlas

**Status:** One zone, Oakhallow, has passed gate 2 of [the zone pipeline](../zone-pipeline.md).

The atlas is how zones fit together. Update it at gate 1 of each new zone, and only with a person's
decision.

## Zones

| Zone | Order | Level range | Travel speed there | Connects to | Kits | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oakhallow, the human starting zone | 1 | 1 to 10 | 6.4 m/s, on foot | The human city, at its far end | Forest biome (today's art), and new fell biome, rural human culture, and Oakhallow signature kits | [Oakhallow](zones/oakhallow.md) |
| The human city | Not decided | Not decided | Not decided | Oakhallow | Not decided | Not designed |

- **Order:** When a typical player reaches it.
- **Travel speed there:** The fastest way of traveling that a typical player has. A zone's length
  in minutes is measured at this speed.
- **Kits:** See "Asset kits" in [docs/assets.md](../assets.md).

## Travel tools

Each tool sets the speed of the zones after it, so add a tool before adding a zone that needs it.

| Tool | Speed | Earned in |
|---|---|---|
| On foot | 6.4 m/s | The start |
| Mounts | Not decided | Not decided |
| Movement speed items | Not decided | Not decided |
| Flight paths and other transport | Not decided | Not decided |

A recall to a saved hub only goes back, so it doesn't change the speed of any zone.
