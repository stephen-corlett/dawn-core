# Zone skeleton

You're doing gate 2 of the zone pipeline. You can't ask anyone anything, and you can't see any
earlier conversation, so work only from the brief you were given.

Read [docs/zone-pipeline.md](../../../docs/zone-pipeline.md), then
[docs/world/bible.md](../../../docs/world/bible.md), then the brief. If the brief's premise hasn't
been passed by a person, report **Blocked** and stop.

## Steps

Work from the outside in. Don't design any sites, quests, or details.

1. **Offer three layouts,** unless the brief's Decisions have chosen one. They need different
   overall shapes, such as a basin around a central sight, a long valley, or a ring around a
   mountain. Draw each as a simple map image, kept outside the repository.
2. **Place from large to small:** the way in and out, the tentpole, then major landmarks, the hub,
   regions, routes, and blocked land. Give each a position in meters and a reason.
3. **Fit the premise.** The land follows the history. Roads go where people had reasons to go, and
   towns stand near water and trade.
4. **Time the routes.** At the zone's travel speed from the atlas, add up route lengths and check
   them against the bible's scale.
5. **Write the sight graph.** For each place, list what can be seen next, and what hides what. Walk
   it from the arrival. Fix any dead end, any place with no next thing, and any junction with only
   one lure.
6. **Check the zone's shape.** Confirm it has an arrival, a middle, and a far end, that each region
   feels different, that the hub leads to several regions, and that a lookout shows the next zone.
7. **Record.** Fill in the Skeleton section for a chosen layout, and add a gate log entry with image
   paths, route times, and anything that misses the bible. Don't change a target to pass it.

Sight lines here are a plan until gate 3 tests them on real terrain. Say so in your entry.

## Report

Report **Needs decision** with the options or finished skeleton. Report **Failed** if no layout
works, and why. A person passes this gate, so never report **Passed**.
